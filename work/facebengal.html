FACEBENGAL

A social and digital platform concept — the idea of a homegrown network, thought through from first principles. Early stage, and labeled exactly as what it is: a concept.

TypeConcept — in development
My roleConcept + direction
ShapeSocial / digital platform
StatusEarly stage

The idea

FaceBengal starts from a simple observation: the biggest social platforms weren't built for every community's context. A concept-stage platform asks — what would a network look like if it grew from local culture outward, instead of being imported inward?

Where it stands

Right now FaceBengal is thinking work: positioning, audience, and what the first version would even need to be. No features are claimed here that don't exist yet — when the product thinking firms up, this page becomes a full case study.

Why show a concept at all

Because the Voxpher rule is honesty over hype. Showing the concept shelf — clearly marked — is more truthful than pretending everything is finished. Some of the best products on this site started exactly here.
