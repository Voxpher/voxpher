Case study · Personal project

NAMASTE

A beat-synced animated film — where the music background and the visual obsession finally became one thing. Concept, direction, animation and edit, all mine.

TypePersonal — animated film
My roleConcept → final cut, solo
Foundation11-page creative blueprint
StatusCompleted

The concept

NAMASTE started as a question: what if a film was composed like a track? Not a video with music laid over it, but visuals built on the beat — cuts landing on drums, motion breathing with the bassline, the whole film mixed like a song.

The blueprint

Before a single frame was animated, the film existed as an 11-page creative blueprint — story, structure, visual language, shot logic, and the exact relationship between sound and image. Animation without a blueprint is decoration; this had a spine before it had skin.

Structure & direction

  • Story — a narrative arc told visually, paced like a musical arrangement: intro, build, drop, resolve.
  • Visual development — a consistent art direction carried across every shot, so the film feels like one world.
  • Animation — motion designed to the rhythm track, not against it.
  • Music relationship — the beat isn't background; it's the edit's metronome.

Why it matters

NAMASTE is the clearest proof of the Voxpher idea: a beat-maker's timing applied to film. It's personal work — no client, no brief — which is exactly why it could be uncompromising.
