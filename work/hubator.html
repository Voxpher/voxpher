Case study · Product

HUBATOR

An online retail and print-on-demand fashion brand — t-shirts, accessories and more. This was never "just a website"; it was an e-commerce ecosystem, built and managed end to end.

TypeProduct — e-commerce brand
My roleBuild + management
StackWooCommerce · WordPress
StatusActive project

The idea

Selling online looks simple from the outside — a store, some products, a checkout button. The reality is a loop: products, presentation, payments, orders, shipping, customer trust, repeat. Hubator was built to run that entire loop, not just to look like a shop.

What was built

  • Storefront — responsive fashion retail experience, designed for browsing on phones first.
  • Product management — catalog structure for t-shirts, accessories and print-on-demand lines, with variants and imagery.
  • Commerce engine — WooCommerce on WordPress, with PHP/MySQL underneath: carts, checkout, payment integration, order management.
  • Operations thinking — shipping integration, transactional emails, customer experience from order to delivery.
  • Performance & infrastructure — optimization for speed, plus hosting, domain and CDN setup.

The honest part

E-commerce is where "design" meets "does it actually work at 2am when someone checks out." Hubator taught the unglamorous half of building products: taxes and shipping rules, failed payments, customer emails, and the discipline of keeping a live store healthy. No revenue figures are published here — the work speaks through the system itself.
