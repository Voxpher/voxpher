About

I'm Papan Sutradhar,
the person behind Voxpher.

Not an agency. Not a team of twenty. One person who got curious about rap in 2018 and simply never stopped learning: design, cameras, editing, code, products.

Intro

Nothing here was planned. Everything here was built.

I started with music, writing raps, making beats on FL Studio on my phone, figuring out how a song looks as much as how it sounds. That curiosity leaked into everything else: graphic design, photography, videography, video editing, animation.

Then came the building side. Websites first, then real software: apps with backends, databases, authentication, maps, payments. The same instinct that made me finish a beat made me finish a product.

Voxpher is the name I put on all of it: the rap identity, the design work, the code, the films. One creative universe, built by one person, where music, visuals and technology keep colliding into new things.

How I think

Curiosity first. Finish what you start.

I don't collect skills like badges. I follow whatever the work demands. If a project needs a beat, I make the beat. If it needs a backend, I build the backend. The through-line is simple: learn it by making it, and ship the thing.

I'd rather show you one finished product than ten certificates. That's why this site leads with work, not words.

Timeline

The evolution.

2018: The start

Rap, writing, beats

Started exploring rap and music. FL Studio on mobile became the lab: songwriting, beat-making, sound, and the visual presentation around it.

Exploration

Design and cameras

Graphic design, photography, videography, video editing. Learning composition, color, timing, and how to finish creative work.

Expansion

Motion and animation

Editing deepened into motion graphics and animation. Wrote the 11-page creative blueprint behind NAMASTE, a beat-synced animated film.

Building

Web and software

WordPress and WooCommerce experiments grew into real development: JavaScript, PHP/MySQL, then Flutter and Dart, Node.js and Express, MongoDB, Firebase, maps and auth systems.

Products

Ideas become businesses

Indoride, a complete ride-booking product designed and built solo. Hubator, an e-commerce brand run end to end. Deepex FF and FaceBengal are new digital bets.

Now

Voxpher, fully

Everything under one roof: music, visuals, code, products. Open to select collaborations, and always building the next thing.

Background

Off the expected path.

I hold a Mechanical Engineering diploma. Not a design or computer-science degree. Everything creative and technical on this site was learned independently, by building. I consider that a feature, not a gap.
