Privacy policy.

Last updated: September 2026

This is a personal portfolio site. It collects very little — here's exactly what, and why.

What I collect

  • Contact form messages. If you use the contact form, you send your name, email address, project type, optional budget range, and your message. That's it. It's delivered to me at hello@voxpher.com so I can reply to you — nothing else happens with it.

What I don't collect

  • No accounts, no logins, no passwords.
  • No analytics or tracking scripts are currently installed on this site.
  • No advertising, no ad cookies, no cross-site tracking.
  • I don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

Technical notes

  • This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may process strictly-necessary technical data (like IP addresses in server logs) to deliver the site securely — that's governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policy.
  • If a form-backend service (currently Formspree, if configured) delivers your message, that service processes it under its own policy.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts; placeholder images load from picsum.photos. Those services may see standard request data when your browser fetches them.

Your rights

Want a copy of a message you sent, or want it deleted? Email hello@voxpher.com and I'll take care of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
