Music

VOXPHER
the artist.

Before the design, before the code, there was rap. Voxpher is my artist identity: writing, beats, and the visuals wrapped around them.

The start: 2018

A phone, FL Studio,
and something to say.

I didn't start in a studio. I started on a phone, making beats in FL Studio mobile, writing bars, learning how drums, bass and voice fit together by trial and error.

Rap taught me the two things everything else runs on: rhythm (timing, pacing, knowing when to hit and when to hold back) and voice (saying something that's actually yours).

The beats got better. The writing got sharper. And somewhere along the way I realized I cared as much about how the music looked (the artwork, the videos, the world around a song) as much as how it sounded. That's the thread that pulled me into design and film.

The craft

Three disciplines, one sound.

01

Rap & songwriting

Written bars first. Flow, wordplay and delivery. The pen is still the starting point of every track.

02

Beat-making

Drums, 808s, melodies and sampling. Built in FL Studio. Started on mobile, grown from there.

03

Visual identity

Artwork, videos and the aesthetic around the music. A song isn't finished until its world exists.

An honest note. This page is the story behind the music, not a discography page with inflated numbers. Releases live on the platforms they're released on; what matters here is the craft and where it led: into design, film, and building products.

Music is still the heartbeat.

Everything I build (a brand, an app, a film) gets the same treatment a track gets: rhythm, structure, and a finish worth releasing.
