Studio

A personal creative universe,
run by one person.

No departments. No hand-offs. The same mind that writes the concept designs the frames, cuts the edit and ships the code.

The setup

Small room.
Full pipeline.

Everything on this site — a beat, a brand film, an app with a live backend — came out of the same workflow: understand it, design it, build it, release it. Working solo isn't a limitation here; it's the reason the work stays coherent.

Principles

What the studio stands on.

01

Truth over hype

I'd rather show one finished product than ten impressive claims. If it's a concept, I say concept. If it's an experiment, I say experiment.

02

Finish the thing

Ideas are cheap; shipped work isn't. A finished beat, a deployed app, a released film — done beats perfect.

03

Learn by making

No skill on this site came from a certificate. Every one came from a project that demanded it.

04

Design is not decoration

How something looks is part of how it works. The interface, the artwork, the edit — all of it serves the idea.

05

Own the stack

Concept, design, code, deployment — one person across the whole chain means nothing gets lost in translation.

06

Stay independent

No agency theater. Direct conversation, honest timelines, work I'm personally proud to sign.

Process

How a project runs.

Step 1

Understand

We talk. I ask uncomfortable questions. We define what "done" actually looks like before anything gets made.

Step 2

Design

Concepts, directions, drafts. You see real options early — not a big reveal at the end.

Step 3

Build

The work gets made — beats, frames, interfaces, code — with regular check-ins, not silence.

Step 4

Launch

Release, deploy, publish. Then we look at what it did and decide what's next.
